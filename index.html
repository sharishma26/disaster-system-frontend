  • Drop, Cover and Hold
  • Stay away from windows
  • Use stairs instead of lifts

🌀 Cyclone Safety

  • Charge mobile phones
  • Store drinking water

🔥 Fire Safety

  • Do not use elevators
  • Call emergency services
